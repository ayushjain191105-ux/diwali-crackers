Green crackers

Items marked Green are CSIR-NEERI approved green crackers with a QR code on the pack. Rules on which crackers can be sold differ by city and change every year, so we only confirm orders that are allowed in your area.

Use crackers safely

  • Light them outdoors in an open space, never in a crowd.
  • Keep a bucket of water and a first aid kit nearby.
  • Adults should light every cracker. Children should watch from a distance.
  • Never relight a cracker that did not go off. Pour water on it.
  • Wear cotton clothes and keep loose fabric away from sparks.
